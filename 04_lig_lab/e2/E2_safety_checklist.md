# E2 safety checklist: tick every box, every session

Session: ☐ S0 ☐ D1 ☐ D2  Date: ______  Operator: ______________  Lab in-charge: ______________

The lab's own rules come first. If this sheet and the lab disagree, follow the lab and write the difference down.

## 1. Before switching on

- ☐ The lab in-charge has given permission for this session and knows the materials: polyimide film, silver paint, IPA.
- ☐ At least two people are present. One of them has been trained on this machine.
- ☐ We know where the **emergency stop**, the **CO₂ fire extinguisher**, the main switch and the first-aid kit are, and have pointed to each.
- ☐ The film's safety data sheet (SDS) has been read. Heated polyimide releases irritating and potentially toxic gases (the SDS lists them). **The exhaust is mandatory.**
- ☐ Exhaust/extraction is ON and pulling: a tissue at the grille is drawn in.
- ☐ Chiller is ON and water is flowing. Water temperature: ____ °C (within the lab's limit). **Never fire a glass tube without cooling water.**
- ☐ The lid interlock works: the job pauses when the lid is opened. Test this once on a frame run with no power.
- ☐ The bed is clear: no paper, tissues or tape rolls inside, and the honeycomb is clean of old debris.
- ☐ The IPA bottle and silver paint are closed and **away from the laser**.
- ☐ The settings are exactly those in `e2_sample_matrix.csv`. Start from the lowest power. Nothing above the plan without the lab in-charge.

## 2. While lasing

- ☐ Someone watches the job the whole time. **Never leave it running unattended, not even "for a minute".**
- ☐ The lid stays closed. Don't lean over the window.
- ☐ **Flame:** press emergency stop, keep the lid closed (a small flame starves), and use the CO₂ extinguisher only if it continues. Tell the lab in-charge.
- ☐ Unusual sound, smell, a mirror or tube problem, or the chiller alarm: emergency stop and call the lab in-charge.
- ☐ Before opening the lid, wait until the smoke has been extracted (at least 30 s after the job ends).

## 3. Handling and measuring

- ☐ Nitrile gloves when handling lased film. Carbon flakes from the tape test are wiped up with a **damp** tissue; never blow them away.
- ☐ Silver paint and IPA are used in a ventilated place, with no flame or laser nearby. Lids go back on straight after use.
- ☐ The multimeter is only used on the coupons and resistors. Never use it on the laser's power supply. **The laser power supply is high voltage. Never open it.**
- ☐ Part W: saline drops on film only, never near the laser or mains equipment. Wipe up spills.

## 4. Before leaving

- ☐ Laser switched off as the lab requires. Exhaust runs a few more minutes. Chiller handled per lab rule.
- ☐ Scrap film and the coupons are bagged and labelled; the waste goes in the bin the lab specifies.
- ☐ Bed and work area cleaned. Gloves and tissues disposed of.
- ☐ Any incident or near-miss is written in the notes of `e2_session.csv` and told to the lab in-charge.
