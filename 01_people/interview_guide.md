# Interview guide: people who care for chronic wounds

**Goal:** understand their day and their problems. **Don't pitch our idea until the very end.** People are polite and will praise any idea, so we learn much more from stories about what they actually did.

## Before you start

- **Say who you are:** "We're engineering students trying to understand wound care before we design anything."
- **Ask permission to take notes.** Use roles, not names (e.g., "Nurse A, district hospital").
- **No photos of patients or wounds** without clear written consent. Photos of dressing materials and the dressing room are fine if staff agree.
- Use whatever language they're comfortable in: Marathi, Hindi or English.
- Aim for about 20 minutes. Ask follow-ups: "Can you tell me more?" and "Why?"

## Questions for nurses

1. Tell me about the **last time** you changed a dressing on a diabetic foot wound or a bedsore. What did you do, step by step?
2. How do you **decide** a dressing needs changing? Is it a fixed schedule, or something you see?
3. How do you tell a wound is **getting infected**? What do you look for, feel for or smell? How early can you tell?
4. What's the **hardest or most frustrating** part of looking after these wounds?
5. How long does one dressing change take? How many patients like this do you see in a day?
6. What happens **between visits**? Who checks the wound at home?
7. What dressings do you use: gauze, foam, silver, vacuum (NPWT)? What do you like or dislike about them? Who pays?
8. If you could know **one thing** about the wound **without opening the dressing**, what would it be?
9. What would stop you from using a new device? Cost, time, training, trust, hygiene?
10. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?

**Only now:** show a rough hand sketch of our idea and ask: *"What would worry you about this?"*

## Extra questions for doctors

- Which signs do you actually trust when judging healing or infection: temperature, exudate amount, smell, pH, size?
- What would a device need to prove before you'd trust its reading?
- Is electrical stimulation used for wounds here? What do you think of it?

## Extra questions for patients or family (with permission)

- How often do you travel for dressing changes? How far, and how much does it cost?
- Does removing the dressing hurt?
- What do you do at home between visits? What worries you?
